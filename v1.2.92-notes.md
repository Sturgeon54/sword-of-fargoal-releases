## What's new in v1.2.92

### Temple oracle — chance-based sword hint
- **[T] temple offerings** of 25g+ now have a **35% chance** to whisper which level holds the Sword
- You may play a full run and **never** learn the exact floor before finding it
- Failed rolls show *"The oracle is silent… for now."*
- **[B]** is potions only — sacrifice hints no longer mention B

Download **Sword-of-Fargoal.zip** and extract before running, or use **Update Now** from v1.2.46+.
