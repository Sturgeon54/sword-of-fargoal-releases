## What's new in v1.3.2

### Custom dungeon art (Tiers 1–3)
- **Floor, walls, chests, fountain, sword, hero, and stairs** — new pixel-art tiles replace procedural/C64 fallbacks where assets are present
- **Seamless walls** — world-aligned brick pattern (no per-tile frame)
- **Temple** — purple shrine with a bold gold cross on the altar
- **Monsters** — 15 custom sprites (direwolf through fyre drake, plus treasure goblin), edge-cleaned for transparent floors
- **Hazards** — pit (O), spike trap (^), and mystery square (`) use new art; pits draw as a hole overlay on the dungeon floor

### Stairs & minimap
- **Dungeon stairs** — gray isometric PNG steps in the playfield
- **Minimap** — cyan down / gold up triangles (sized like v1.2.92, not oversized)

### Gameplay fix
- **New game** — hero no longer spawns on the down-stairs tile

Download **Sword-of-Fargoal.zip** and extract before running, or use **Update Now** from v1.2.46+.
