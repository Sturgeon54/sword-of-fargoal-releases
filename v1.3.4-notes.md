## What's new in v1.3.4

### Dry fountain sprite
- Used fountains now show a **dry basin** instead of glowing blue water
- Dry art is derived from the filled fountain — same stone structure, moss, and gargoyle head; only the water and sparkles are removed
- Minimap dot turns **gray** after you drink from a fountain
